🧬 Biological Connectome Pathways

  • Feeding Reflex (SEZ $\to$ MN9): 443 real neurons and 20,281 synapses from the FlyWire v630 female Drosophila connectome (Shiu et al., Nature 2024). Driven by biophysical Leaky Integrate-and-Fire equations ($V_{rest} = -52\text{ mV}$, $V_{th} = -45\text{ mV}$, $1.8\text{ ms}$ synaptic delay).
  • Compass Ring Attractor (Central Complex): 16-wedge Ellipsoid Body (E-PG) neural compass maintaining an activity bump that continuously tracks the fly's allocentric heading direction (Seelig & Jayaraman, Nature 2015).
  • Descending Locomotion (DNa01/02 & MDN): Bilateral antennal scent gradients drive asymmetric steering via DNa01/02 descending neurons; contact with bitter quinine depolarizes MDN (Moonwalker Descending Neurons) to command backward walking (Bidaye et al., Science 2014).

🎮 Habitat Scaffolding & Presentation Layer

  • Tripod Gait Kinematics: 6-legged alternating tripod stride driven by coupled kinematic phase oscillators, representing the thoracic Central Pattern Generators (CPGs) of the ventral nerve cord.
  • Fluid Dynamics: Procedural surface-tension droplets with realistic volume depletion and specular meniscus reflections upon ingestion.

🔬 Biological Connectome Architecture

Extracted from the full adult female Drosophila melanogaster brain connectome (FlyWire v630, 139,255 neurons, >50 million synapses). This subcircuit represents the minimum complete closed loop required for gustatory sensorimotor transformation.

Biophysical Leaky Integrate-and-Fire Dynamics

τ_m · (dV_m / dt) = -(V_m - V_0) + g_syn(t)
τ_syn · (dg_syn / dt) = -g_syn + Σ w_ij · δ(t - t_spike - t_delay)
• Resting potential ($V_0$): -52.0 mV
• Spike threshold ($V_{th}$): -45.0 mV
• Membrane timescale ($\tau_m$): 20.0 ms
• Synaptic decay ($\tau$): 5.0 ms
• Refractory period: 2.2 ms
• Synaptic delay: 1.8 ms (18 steps)

Cellular Distribution (443 Neurons)

  • 21 Sugar Sensory Afferents (Gr5a): Depolarize upon sucrose contact on tarsal or labellar hairs.
  • 21 Bitter Sensory Afferents (Gr66a): Depolarize upon quinine / bitter contact.
  • 399 Subesophageal (SEZ) Interneurons: Excitatory cholinergic and inhibitory GABAergic interneurons that mediate feedforward excitation and lateral reciprocal inhibition.
  • 2 Motor Neurons (MN9-1 & MN9-2): Innervate the rostrum protractor muscle (M9), triggering extension of the proboscis to sip food.
